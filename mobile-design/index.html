9:41Game brief
The expression parade

Emoji Gates

Help your little hero pass six gates by matching the requested expression.

How to play

Follow one cue at a time. Open your mouth, smile with your lips closed, or bare your upper and lower teeth comfortably. Return to neutral between expressions.

During the game

The next action appears above the game. Follow the changing instruction as you pass each gate.

This design preview simulates matches. No facial or sound detection is active.
