Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Face Chaos handles information when you play its Android camera-overlay games, record clips, share videos or contact support.

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · App: com.facechaos.mobile

At a glance

  • Facial-expression and head-movement detection runs on your device.
  • Sound-level and rhythm controls process microphone samples locally.
  • Optional country-name speech recognition may use your phone’s online speech provider.
  • Recordings, game records, preferences and in-app prototype ratings are stored on your phone.
  • You decide whether to save a recording to your Gallery or send it to another app.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the Face Chaos Android app and this privacy-policy website. For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact saheedseunpeter@gmail.com.

2. Camera, facial expressions and head movement

With your permission, Face Chaos uses the front camera to show your live preview and, when facial or head controls are enabled, to detect the gestures needed to play. The app processes camera frames using a model bundled with the app to calculate facial landmarks, expression measurements and head position.

These recognition calculations run on your device. Recognition frames and measurements are used temporarily in memory; the app does not upload them to a Face Chaos server, retain a face template, identify who you are or use your face for advertising. A sensitivity preference may be saved locally. Camera capture stops when the app goes into the background.

Camera images are saved only when you choose to record a gameplay video. Such a video can contain your face, your surroundings and the game overlay; see the recordings section.

3. Microphone and optional speech recognition

Sound-level and rhythm games

With microphone permission, the app measures sound level, strength, duration and pauses to control games. These microphone samples are processed on the phone and discarded rather than saved as audio files or sent to a Face Chaos server. Microphone capture stops when the game is paused or the app goes into the background.

Country-name answers

Speech recognition is different from sound-level control. When you choose to say a country name, Face Chaos asks Android’s installed speech-recognition service to turn your answer into text. The app prefers on-device recognition when available, but may fall back to the phone’s default service. That service may send audio to its own servers.

The speech service’s processing, retention and privacy terms depend on the provider installed on your phone. Review that provider’s settings and privacy policy. Face Chaos uses the returned words to check the current answer and does not save a transcript to its own server. You can type an answer instead of using speech. Country-name answers currently use English.

4. Gameplay recordings and Gallery copies

Recording is optional. When you enable it, Face Chaos saves an MP4 containing the camera image and game overlay in app-specific storage on your phone. Current gameplay recordings are silent and do not contain an audio track.

Use My recordings to review, share or delete a local clip. Choosing Save to Gallery creates a separate copy in Movies / Face Chaos, or a location you choose on older Android versions. Your Gallery or device-backup provider may manage exported copies under its own settings.

5. Local game information

The app stores settings and progress locally, including language and input choices, sensitivity, sound preferences, earned coins, stage unlocks, per-game records, and references to saved clips. Optional in-app prototype ratings, completed-round counts and rating-reminder preferences are also local. Those prototype ratings are not automatically sent to Google Play or to the developer.

Face Chaos does not require an account. The current app does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics tracking and does not ask for your contacts, precise location or advertising identifier.

6. Sharing and other services

Nothing is posted automatically. If you choose to share a clip, Android grants your selected app access to that clip. TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp or another recipient may then process the video, your account information and your posting choices under its own privacy policy. Face Chaos cannot control copies retained by those services or by other people after you share them.

The app’s local expression detection and sound-level controls do not require a Face Chaos online account. Online speech recognition, social sharing, app distribution and exported-media backups may involve providers outside Face Chaos.

7. Support messages

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue or handle your privacy concern. Please do not send private recordings or other sensitive information unless needed for your request. The email service also processes correspondence under its own terms.

8. Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Recognition data: facial measurements, recognition frames and sound-control samples are temporary processing data, not a stored identity profile.
  • Local clips: remain in app-specific storage until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app.
  • Gallery copies: remain until deleted separately in your Gallery or file manager. Deleting the app’s original does not delete exported copies.
  • Settings, coins, records and local ratings: remain until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Clearing storage also removes app-local recordings.
  • External copies and speech processing: retention and deletion are controlled by the relevant social, speech, Gallery or backup provider.
  • Support correspondence: is kept as needed to handle the request and applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may ask us to delete it using the contact address below.

You can revoke camera and microphone permissions in Android Settings, use available alternative controls, turn recording off, choose typed country answers, and decide whether to export or share videos. Features that need a revoked permission will be unavailable. There is no Face Chaos account to delete and no developer-held copy of your local game progress or recordings for us to retrieve.

9. Security

App-specific storage is protected by Android’s application-access controls, and video sharing uses permission-limited, read-only access to the selected clip. Face Chaos disables Android app-data backup for its app-specific data. Exported videos and data held by other services follow those services’ protections and your device settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children and families

Face Chaos does not request a player’s name, date of birth or school information. Parents and caregivers should supervise camera use, recordings and sharing, and review the age requirements of any external service a player chooses. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support inbox without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can review and address the request.
