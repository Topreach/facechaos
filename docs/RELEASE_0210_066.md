## Changes since version 53

- Welcome page and five bundled interface languages.
- Canvas Look and Share panels with native accessible controls and RTL layout.
- Stronger optional camera brightening/warmth and smoothing on supported devices, momentary original comparison, camera-only effects in recordings; recognition uses original pixels.
- Continuous recording across retries and stages, with pause/resume and Gallery export.
- Teeth-only corrections for nearly closed jaws, partial front-tooth bands and warm ivory colour. Closed-lip smile rules remain unchanged.
- R8 optimization, updated Fragment and adaptive orientation/resizing.
