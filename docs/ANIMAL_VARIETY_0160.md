# Twelve-animal Animal Choir - 0.16.0

The player requested more variety, retaining the previously chosen rhythm-matching mechanic. The choir now includes dog, cat, bird, cow, sheep, duck, owl, frog, rooster, lion, monkey and elephant. Each has a named call, a short/long reference pattern, a generated cartoon character and an original synthetic Hear pattern guide. These are human-playable cartoon rhythms, not real animal recordings or animal-species classification.

Patterns: dog = two short woofs; cat = one longer meow; bird = three short tweets; cow = two longer moos; sheep = long baa then short ba; duck = three medium quacks; owl = two medium hoots; frog = short/long/short ribbits; rooster = three short syllables then a longer doo; lion = long roar then two short calls; monkey = four short oo calls; elephant = long/short/long trumpet syllables. No particular pitch or shouting is required: recognition checks call count, duration and pauses.

Stage call counts are 6, 8, 10, 12 and 12. Each stage has a distinct order with no repeated animal within that stage; all twelve appear across the campaign, and the last two stages include all twelve. Early stages permit one missed call pattern; the final stage requires all twelve with its existing one-life challenge. The previous fixed 80-world-second cap is removed for Animal Choir because every cue already has its own deadline and the expanded finale must have enough total time. Other games keep their existing time limits. Shared stage speed multipliers, Stage 1 retries, best coin records, microphone calibration freeze and paused example playback are preserved.

Artwork uses a new transparent atlas for all twelve animals, leaving other games' original animal/lantern assets untouched. The current animal occupies the centre of the game, with neighboring animals on either side. Long calls have wider beat markers, current progress fills the relevant marker, and the title names the animal. The briefing displays all twelve and provides guide buttons in rows; the in-game Hear pattern button follows the current cue. All existing raw three-animal lookup assumptions in gameplay and rendering were replaced by AnimalPatterns metadata. Game-area dimensions, camera quality and face calibration are unchanged.

Validation: twelve unique animal names/reference patterns, twelve non-silent guides, no duplicate animal per stage and full campaign coverage passed. All 48 campaign cues passed through synthetic PCM, SoundGate and the production rhythm rules to complete all five stages. Calibration freeze, brief-dropout tolerance, wrong patterns, demo isolation and scoring checks passed. Existing 75-stage winning paths, 70 touch-compatible paths, winning/losing game checks and increasing-stage-speed checks passed. Android build and lint passed. On Samsung SM-A176B, 48 synthetic animal/calibration/progress/touch scenes and guide hit targets passed without microphone or speaker use. The elephant scene was visually reviewed; the generated atlas was inspected. Real microphone and speaker-guide experience still needs the user's live test.
